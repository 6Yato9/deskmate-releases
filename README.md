# Deskmate

A pixel raccoon that lives on your desktop and helps you through the day: a Pomodoro timer, stand-up and other reminders, and a daily planner that picks your next task. Everything stays on your computer.

Made by [Zero Horizon](https://www.zerohorizon.studio/works).
